Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect the financial data you choose to log, including expense amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may additionally collect basic technical information, such as your browser type and general usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide and maintain the Service, including storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and authenticating your account. We may also use your email address to send you important account-related notices. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never kept as plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on third-party services for functions such as hosting, fonts, and infrastructure. These providers may process limited technical data as part of delivering the Service, but they do not have access to your financial records beyond what is necessary to operate the underlying infrastructure. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We are happy to address any privacy-related questions you may have.
